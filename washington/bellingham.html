Medicare in Bellingham

What to know before you compare plans in Whatcom County

Bellingham is the medical center for the northwest corner of the state, and its care map is compact: PeaceHealth St. Joseph Medical Center is the hospital, the Family Care Network is the largest independent primary-care group, and Skagit Regional Health in Mount Vernon is the next system south. Whatcom County’s Advantage menu is moderate, and the network question is whether a plan includes both PeaceHealth and the Seattle specialists that St. Joseph refers out to.

Bellingham also has a large population of retirees who moved here from elsewhere and a steady share who split time with a place in Arizona or on the islands; a Medigap policy covers all of it without a network, and in Washington it can be changed at any time. Veterans use the VA Puget Sound clinic in Bellingham and Mount Vernon.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Already have a Medigap policy? Washington lets you change it any time of year.

Questions from Bellingham

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Bellingham?

Whatcom County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Washington, and the full Part D lineup. Availability is set by county and changes each plan year; we compare what is sold at your address.

Is PeaceHealth St. Joseph in my plan?

PeaceHealth contracts with some Advantage plans and not others, and the list changes each fall. A Medigap policy with Original Medicare covers St. Joseph and any Seattle referral without a network question. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

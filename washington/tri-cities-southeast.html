The Tri-Cities & Southeast Washington: what shapes the choice here

The Tri-Cities are one of the fastest-growing retirement destinations in the state, and the plan menu is moderate and specific: Kadlec in Richland is a Providence hospital, Trios in Kennewick and Lourdes in Pasco are separately owned, and each contracts on its own terms with the Advantage carriers. Hanford retirees with employer coverage and a growing Apple Health population in Franklin County add two more layers.

East and south, Walla Walla has Providence St. Mary and the Wainwright VA; Pullman, Colfax, Clarkston, Pomeroy and Dayton have one hospital apiece and long drives for anything beyond it. Advantage menus there are short, and Original Medicare with a Medigap policy — accepted at every hospital in the region and in Spokane — is often the practical choice.

Local plans to know: The Tri-Cities have three hospitals under three owners — Kadlec (Providence), Trios and Lourdes — and an Advantage plan built on one may not include the others. Walla Walla’s VA medical center serves veterans across the southeast; the Palouse and Blue Mountain counties rely on small hospitals with Spokane or the Tri-Cities for specialty care.

Counties: Benton, Franklin, Walla Walla, Columbia, Garfield, Asotin, Whitman.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Already have a Medigap policy? Washington lets you change it any time of year.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in the Tri-Cities?

Benton and Franklin counties typically offer a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Washington, and the full Part D lineup. Walla Walla, Columbia, Garfield, Asotin and Whitman counties have fewer Advantage plans. We compare what is sold at your address.

Is Kadlec in my Advantage plan?

Kadlec, Trios and Lourdes each contract with some Advantage plans and not others, and the lists change every fall. A Medigap policy with Original Medicare covers all three. We confirm your doctors before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

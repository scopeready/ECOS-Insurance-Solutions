Medicare in Sedona, Arizona

Famous for its red rocks, Sedona and the Verde Valley are a favorite for active retirees.

Sedona and the Verde Valley have a smaller set of plans than the big cities, which makes it especially important to confirm that your doctors and prescriptions are covered before you enroll. I help local residents compare every option available in the area and choose coverage that travels well if you split time between Sedona and another home.

Your options in Sedona

  • Medicare Advantage (Part C) — all-in-one plans, often $0 or low premium, with dental, vision, and drug coverage built in.
  • Medicare Supplement (Medigap) — pairs with Original Medicare for nationwide freedom and low surprise costs.
  • Part D — prescription drug coverage to fit your medication list.

Local care in Sedona

Major health systems serving the Verde Valley (Yavapai & Coconino counties) include Northern Arizona Healthcare - Sedona, Verde Valley Medical Center (Cottonwood). I'll make sure the plan you choose works with the providers you already trust.

Sedona Medicare questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Sedona?

Sedona residents in the Verde Valley (Yavapai & Coconino counties) can typically choose from Medicare Advantage plans, Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans, and standalone Part D drug plans. The specific plans and premiums vary by ZIP code — I can pull the exact list available at your address.

Will my doctors and hospital be covered?

That depends on the plan. Major facilities in the area include Northern Arizona Healthcare - Sedona, Verde Valley Medical Center (Cottonwood). Before you enroll, I confirm your doctors, preferred hospital, and prescriptions are covered so there are no surprises.

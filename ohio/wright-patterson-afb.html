Medicare near Wright-Patterson AFB (Dayton)

Why the Medicare choice is different for a Wright-Patterson family

Greene and Montgomery counties hold a large community of Air Force retirees and their spouses, and most of them reach 65 with TRICARE For Life waiting. TFL requires Medicare Parts A and B and then pays secondary to Medicare, so a Medigap policy is usually unnecessary; its pharmacy is creditable, so a Part D plan usually is too. What is left to decide is whether an MA-only Advantage plan adds dental, vision or hearing worth having — without duplicating the drug coverage you already have.

Veterans without TRICARE use the Dayton VA Medical Center, and here the Part B timing question is the one that matters: VA care is not creditable for Part B, so delaying Part B because you have the VA triggers a lifelong penalty. Wright-Patterson Medical Center, run by the 88th Medical Group, continues to see retirees under TRICARE rules, and the base’s Retiree Activities Office works with TRICARE and the Dayton VA on exactly these questions; Medicare does not pay at a military hospital.

Questions from Wright-Patterson AFB (Dayton) families

Frequently asked questions

I retired from the Air Force with TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit; we check whether one is worth it for you.

I use the Dayton VA. Do I still need Part B?

In most cases, yes. VA medical care is not creditable coverage for Part B, so delaying it can trigger a permanent penalty and leaves you without coverage at a non-VA hospital. Enroll on time.

Can I keep using Wright-Patterson Medical Center after 65?

Retirees can be seen at Wright-Patterson Medical Center under TRICARE rules; Medicare does not pay there. Most Wright-Patt retirees pair TFL with civilian care at Kettering Health or Premier Health, and the Retiree Activities Office (937-257-3221) can help with base questions.

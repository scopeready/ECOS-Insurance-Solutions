Medicare in Akron

What to know before you compare plans in Summit County

Summit County is one of the four Ohio counties where KFF counted 80 or more Medicare Advantage plans for 2025, and it has every Medigap carrier in Ohio and the full Part D lineup. Summa Health, now part of HATCo, and Cleveland Clinic Akron General contract with different carriers, and SummaCare, owned by Summa, sells plans built on that system across 25 northern Ohio counties.

Akron’s Goodyear, Bridgestone and steel retirees carry union and employer plans with their own Medicare rules, and Summit County residents often use Cleveland specialists too. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code, confirm your doctors and pharmacy, and set Advantage beside a Medigap policy.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Akron

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Akron?

Summit County typically offers one of the deepest Medicare Advantage menus in Ohio, including SummaCare and Medical Mutual, every Medigap carrier in the state, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Are Summa and Akron General in the same plans?

Not always. Each contracts with some Advantage carriers and not others, and SummaCare is built on Summa. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network to check. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

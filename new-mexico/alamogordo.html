Medicare in Alamogordo

What to know before you compare plans in Otero County

Otero County offers a moderate Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in New Mexico and the full Part D lineup. Gerald Champion Regional Medical Center, a 99-bed hospital that became the first in the country shared by active-duty military and civilians, is the basin’s hospital; CHRISTUS Health’s acquisition of it took effect July 1, 2026, and its Advantage contracts are worth re-checking this fall. Las Cruces and El Paso are the referral cities.

Holloman’s retirees reach 65 with TRICARE For Life, which usually makes a Medigap policy and a Part D plan unnecessary; our Holloman AFB page walks through it. The Mescalero Apache reservation to the northeast is served by an IHS facility, and our rural New Mexico guide covers how Medicare fits with it.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period, and from January 1, 2027, New Mexico adds a 60-day Medigap birthday window.

Questions from Alamogordo

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Alamogordo?

Otero County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in New Mexico, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

I have TRICARE For Life from Holloman. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit. See our Holloman AFB page.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to work with ECOS Medicare Solutions?

Nothing. Independent Medicare agents are paid by the insurance carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans, answering questions and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you. Your premium is the same whether you enroll through us, another agent or the carrier directly.

Presbyterian is ending my Medicare Advantage plan. What now?

Your coverage runs through December 31, 2026. In June 2026 Presbyterian announced it will discontinue most of its Advantage plans for 2027 (about 30,000 members), keeping only its Dual Plus plan for people who also have Medicaid. You choose new coverage during the Annual Election Period, October 15 to December 7, and because you are losing the plan through no fault of your own you generally also get a guaranteed-issue right to buy a Medigap policy without health questions, for a limited window around the date the coverage ends. Call before the deadline on your notice.

Does New Mexico have a Medigap birthday rule?

Starting January 1, 2027, yes. Senate Bill 21 (2026) gives people who already have a Medigap policy a 60-day window each year, beginning on the first day of their birthday month, to switch to a Medigap plan of equal or lesser benefits without medical underwriting. Until then, your six-month open enrollment at 65 and guaranteed-issue events are the windows that count.

I am under 65 on Medicare because of a disability. Can I buy Medigap in New Mexico?

New Mexico does not require insurers to sell Medigap to people under 65, and the Office of Superintendent of Insurance confirmed none were doing so voluntarily as of 2026. The New Mexico Medical Insurance Pool offers a Medicare Carve-Out Plan to under-65 beneficiaries who have both Part A and Part B. You get a full Medigap open enrollment for every plan at 65.

Does a Medicare Advantage plan cover me at UNM, Presbyterian, Lovelace or Christus St. Vincent?

Only if that hospital and your doctors are in the plan’s network, which is set by the plan and changes yearly. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network: any hospital in New Mexico or across the line in El Paso, Lubbock or Denver that accepts Medicare is covered. We confirm your providers in writing before you enroll.

I live an hour or more from a hospital. Which plan makes sense?

Often Original Medicare with a Medigap policy, because it has no network and covers the county hospital, the Albuquerque specialist and an out-of-state referral the same way. An Advantage plan can work if its network genuinely reaches the providers you use; we check the nearest in-network specialists, not just whether the plan is sold in your county. Our rural New Mexico guide walks through it.

I get my care through the IHS or a tribal clinic. Do I need Medicare?

The Indian Health Service says it is not health insurance and that its Purchased/Referred Care program is the payer of last resort, and it encourages eligible tribal members to enroll in Medicare. Enrolling does not affect your IHS eligibility; Medicare pays the facility for your care and covers care the facility cannot provide. Enroll in Part B on time, because the late penalty lasts for life.

I have TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating the drug benefit. Our Kirtland, Holloman and Cannon pages walk through it.

I use the VA. Do I still need Medicare Part B?

In most cases, yes. VA medical care is not creditable coverage for Part B, so delaying it triggers a permanent penalty and leaves you without coverage at a non-VA hospital, which in most of New Mexico is the only hospital nearby. VA pharmacy is creditable for Part D.

When can I enroll in or change my Medicare plan in New Mexico?

Your Initial Enrollment Period is the seven months around your 65th birthday. The Annual Election Period runs October 15 to December 7; Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 to March 31. Moving counties, losing a plan, or qualifying for Medicaid opens a Special Enrollment Period, and from 2027 Medigap policyholders get a 60-day birthday window each year.

What are the 2026 Medicare costs?

Part B premium $202.90 a month, Part B deductible $283, Part A hospital deductible $1,736 per benefit period, Part D out-of-pocket cap $2,100. Higher earners pay IRMAA surcharges above $109,000 (single) or $218,000 (joint) of 2024 income. Our costs page has the full chart and calculators.

Can New Mexico help pay my Part B premium?

Possibly. New Mexico’s Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, QI), run by the Health Care Authority, pay the Part B premium for people with limited income, and QMB also covers Medicare’s deductibles and copays. New Mexico eliminated the asset test for these programs in 2021, so only income counts. Apply through HCA, or call New Mexico SHIP at 800-432-2080.

What is Turquoise Care?

New Mexico’s Medicaid managed-care program, run by the Health Care Authority since July 1, 2024, when it replaced Centennial Care. Members choose one of four health plans — Blue Cross and Blue Shield of New Mexico, Presbyterian, Molina or UnitedHealthcare Community Plan — which coordinates the Medicaid side, including long-term services and supports, while Medicare pays first for medical care. Several of those companies also offer Dual Special Needs Plans on the Medicare side.

Where can I get free, unbiased Medicare counseling in New Mexico?

New Mexico SHIP, the State Health Insurance Assistance Program, is run by the Aging and Long-Term Services Department’s Aging and Disability Resource Center: 800-432-2080 statewide, or 505-476-4846 in Santa Fe. You can also call 1-800-MEDICARE or use Medicare.gov. We are an independent agency, not a government program, and we say so on every page.

No. We represent a number of insurance organizations and products in New Mexico, not all of them, and we will always say so. For the complete list, use Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE or New Mexico SHIP. For help choosing among the plans we do offer, call (702) 706-6564.

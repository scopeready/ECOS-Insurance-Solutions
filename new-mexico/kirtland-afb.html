Medicare near Kirtland AFB (Albuquerque)

Why the Medicare choice is different for a Kirtland family

Bernalillo County holds one of the largest military-retiree communities in the Southwest, and most of them reach 65 with TRICARE For Life waiting. TFL requires Medicare Parts A and B and then pays secondary to Medicare, so a Medigap policy is usually unnecessary; its pharmacy is creditable, so a Part D plan usually is too. What is left to decide is whether an MA-only Advantage plan adds dental, vision or hearing worth having — without duplicating the drug coverage you already have — and, this year, whether the plan you pick will still be sold in 2027, since Presbyterian is leaving most of that market.

Veterans without TRICARE use the Raymond G. Murphy VA Medical Center on San Pedro, and here the Part B timing question is the one that matters: VA care is not creditable for Part B, so delaying Part B because you have the VA triggers a lifelong penalty. The 377th Medical Group is an outpatient clinic that sees retirees on a space-available basis; it is not a substitute for Medicare.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. A plan leaving your county opens a Special Enrollment Period, and from January 1, 2027, New Mexico adds a 60-day Medigap birthday window.

Questions from Kirtland AFB (Albuquerque) families

Frequently asked questions

I retired from the Air Force with TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit; we check whether one is worth it for you, and whether it will still be sold in 2027.

I use the Albuquerque VA. Do I still need Part B?

In most cases, yes. VA medical care is not creditable coverage for Part B, so delaying it can trigger a permanent penalty and leaves you without coverage at a non-VA hospital. Enroll on time.

Can I keep using the Kirtland clinic after 65?

Retirees can be seen at the 377th Medical Group on a space-available basis under TRICARE rules; Medicare does not pay there, and it is a clinic, not a hospital. Most Kirtland retirees pair TFL with civilian care at Presbyterian, UNM or Lovelace.

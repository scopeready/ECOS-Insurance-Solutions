This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

Static marketing/lead-generation site for ECOS Medicare Solutions (agent: Darin Weidauer, NPN 18580338) serving Colorado, live at https://coloradomedicareenrollment.com. There is no framework, package manager, build step, test suite, or linter in this repo — it holds hand-editable HTML plus shared assets, generated by a small set of Python build scripts (kept outside this repo) that share a template with the Nevada, Arizona and Georgia ECOS sites.

## History

This site was migrated to GitHub/Vercel in September 2026 from SiteGround. The SiteGround export already contained a working, on-brand core site (home, about, contact, FAQ, Medicare Basics, Medicare Advantage vs. Supplement, Medigap G vs. N, penalty calculator, IRMAA, veterans guide, enroll, thank-you, privacy, 404) with real 2026 CMS figures and Darin's real bio/branding — that copy was preserved. What was added in the migration, to bring this site to feature parity with the more mature `Nevada-Medicare-Enrollment` sibling repo:

- `medicare-costs.html`, `part-d.html`, `turning-65.html`, `medicaid.html` — new educational pages
- `areas-we-serve.html` — all 64 Colorado counties grouped by region, plus a city-guide index
- 24 city landing pages (`medicare-<city>-co.html`) spanning the Front Range, Western Slope, San Luis Valley, southern Colorado, the mountain corridor and the eastern plains
- `terms.html`, JSON-LD structured data (WebPage/InsuranceAgency/Person/BreadcrumbList/FAQPage), a skip-to-content link, and a "reviewed by" byline on educational pages — matching Nevada's pattern
- `og-image.png` and `favicon.svg` (favicon and `analytics.js` are the literal state-agnostic files from the Nevada repo — they contain no state-specific content)
- Regenerated `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `robots.txt` covering the full page set

**No DSNP / C-SNP / I-SNP pages exist for Colorado.** Nevada has per-county Dual Special Needs Plan pages; Colorado does not, because there was no verified Colorado SNP carrier/availability data at migration time. Don't add SNP pages by copying Nevada's without real Colorado data — a wrong SNP availability claim is a compliance problem, not just a content gap. There is also no Colorado "Medigap birthday rule" page — unlike Nevada, Colorado does not have a state-specific Medigap birthday/anniversary underwriting rule; do not add one without a citation to a specific Colorado statute or DOI bulletin.

## Deployment

- Hosted on **Vercel**, auto-deploying from GitHub. `vercel.json` sets `cleanUrls: false` and `trailingSlash: false` — pages are served at their literal `.html` filenames (e.g. `/medicare-denver-co.html`), matching every internal link in the repo. Do not switch this to clean URLs without rewriting every internal href and the sitemap.
- Internal links are **relative, with the `.html` suffix** (`medicare-basics.html`, not `/medicare-basics` or `/medicare-basics.html`). Canonical URLs in each page's `<head>` are absolute (`https://coloradomedicareenrollment.com/medicare-basics.html`).

## Every page's shape

Every current page shares: a top bar (phone + NPN), sticky header with logo + nav + call CTA, a `hero`/`hero hero-sm` section, JSON-LD (`WebPage`, an `InsuranceAgency`+`Person` graph, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` where the page has an FAQ), a lead-capture form posting to Web3Forms, a bottom CTA, and the shared footer (nav columns, sibling-site cross-links, TPMO disclaimer). Educational and city pages additionally carry a "Written and reviewed by Darin Weidauer" byline block. Preserve this shape when adding pages — copy the head/nav/footer/JSON-LD wiring from a similar existing page rather than inventing a new layout.

## Contact form

Every lead form on this site POSTs natively to `https://api.web3forms.com/submit` with a `botcheck` honeypot and a required `consent` checkbox (full TCPA consent text in a hidden `consent_text` field, with a JS-stamped `consent_timestamp` for record-keeping). Do not remove the consent checkbox or weaken its wording. The lead-magnet download forms (`handleDownload()` in each page's inline script) POST via `fetch` and reveal a download link on success — they do not use `redirect`, unlike the two navigation-style forms (home/contact hero forms) which redirect to `thank-you.html`.

## Key conventions

- **Pages are self-contained.** Header, nav, footer, disclaimers, and JSON-LD are duplicated in every HTML file. Any site-wide change (phone number, NPN, footer links, disclaimer wording) must be applied to every current page — grep across all `.html` files and verify with a count.
- **SEO/AI-discovery files must stay in sync with the page set**: when adding, renaming, or removing a page, update `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, and `llms-full.txt`.
- Every current page has a canonical tag, meta description, Open Graph/Twitter tags, and JSON-LD structured data.

## Compliance — do not weaken

This is a Medicare marketing site subject to CMS/TPMO rules:

- Every page carries the TPMO disclaimer ("...is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or the federal Medicare program..."). Preserve this wording site-wide.
- Pages cite specific plan-year dollar figures (Part B premium, deductibles, Part D cap, IRMAA brackets). These are the same verified 2026 CMS figures used on the Nevada site — do not invent or "update" these numbers; only change them when the user supplies verified figures for the new plan year, and keep `llms.txt`/`llms-full.txt` and every page that cites them consistent.
- 1-800-MEDICARE is referenced as the official fallback; the business phone is (720) 295-2611, and the site claims licensure across all 64 Colorado counties (not a specific physical office address — none is published on this site).
- Colorado's Medicaid program is **Health First Colorado**, referenced on `medicaid.html`. Don't rename it or attribute Nevada- or Arizona-specific program mechanics to it.

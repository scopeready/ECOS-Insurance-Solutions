What to know before you compare plans in Salt Lake County

West-side residents have Salt Lake County’s broad Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Utah and the full Part D lineup. Jordan Valley (Holy Cross), Intermountain Medical Center and St. Mark’s each contract differently, and many west-side families qualify for both Medicare and Utah Medicaid, which makes Dual Special Needs Plans part of the conversation.

We compare what is sold at your ZIP code, confirm your doctors and pharmacy, and check whether a Medicare Savings Program applies.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving counties opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in West Valley City?

The full Salt Lake County lineup: a wide Medicare Advantage menu including Dual Special Needs Plans, every Medigap carrier in Utah, and the full Part D lineup.

Can Utah Medicaid help pay my Part B premium?

Possibly. Utah’s Medicare Savings Programs pay the Part B premium for people with limited income and resources; apply through the Department of Workforce Services. See our Utah Medicaid page.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

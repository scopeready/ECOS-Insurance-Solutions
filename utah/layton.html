When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving counties opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Layton

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Layton?

Davis County typically offers a broad Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Utah, and the full Part D lineup. Availability changes each plan year.

I retired from Hill AFB with TRICARE For Life. Do I need Medigap or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision. See our Hill AFB page.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

How & When to Enroll in Medicare

Understanding when to enroll is just as important as what to enroll in. Missing your window can mean permanent penalties.

Medicare Enrollment Timeline

1
3 Months Before Age 65

Initial Enrollment Period (IEP) Begins

Your IEP is a 7-month window. Sign up at ssa.gov, call Social Security (1-800-772-1213), or visit your local SSA office.

2
Your Birthday Month

Coverage Can Begin

Enroll in the first 3 months = coverage starts your birthday month. Later = delayed start.

3
If You Have Employer Coverage

Special Enrollment Period (SEP)

Employer group coverage lets you delay Part B without penalty. When it ends, you get an 8-month SEP. COBRA and Marketplace do NOT count.

4
January 1 – March 31 Each Year

General Enrollment Period (GEP)

Missed your IEP? Sign up during GEP. Coverage starts July 1. Late penalties may apply.

5
After Enrolling in A & B

Choose: Advantage or Supplement

Add Medigap + Part D, or switch to Medicare Advantage. We compare every option free.

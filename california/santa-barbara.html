Medicare in Santa Barbara

What to know before you compare plans in Santa Barbara County

Santa Barbara offers a moderate Advantage menu built on Cottage Health and Sansum Clinic (now part of Sutter), every Medigap carrier in California and the full Part D lineup. Many residents choose Original Medicare with a Medigap policy for unrestricted access to Cottage plus UCLA or Cedars for specialty care, and re-shop the premium each year under the birthday rule.

We compare what is sold at your ZIP code, confirm your doctors and medical group, and set Advantage beside a Medigap policy so the trade-off is plain.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31, and California’s Medigap birthday rule gives you 60 days from your birthday each year to switch supplements without health questions.

Questions from Santa Barbara

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Santa Barbara?

Santa Barbara County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in California, and the full Part D lineup. Availability is set by county and changes each plan year.

Will my Santa Barbara doctors take a Medicare Advantage plan?

Each system contracts with some Medicare Advantage plans and not others, and the lists change every October. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network to check, except Kaiser, which is reached only through Kaiser Senior Advantage. We confirm your doctors before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

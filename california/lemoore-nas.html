Medicare near NAS Lemoore & Edwards Air Force Base

Why the Medicare choice is different for a Lemoore, Edwards or China Lake family

Hanford, Lemoore, Lancaster, Palmdale and Ridgecrest hold thousands of retirees who reach 65 with TRICARE For Life waiting. TFL requires Medicare Parts A and B and then pays secondary to Medicare, so a Medigap policy is usually unnecessary; its pharmacy is creditable, so a Part D plan usually is too. What is left is whether a local Advantage plan’s network reaches the specialists you need in Fresno, Bakersfield or Los Angeles, which is the real question in these towns.

Veterans without TRICARE use VA Central California in Fresno, the VA Loma Linda system and its Lancaster and Ridgecrest clinics, and here the Part B timing question is the one that matters: VA care is not creditable for Part B. The Lemoore and Edwards clinics see retirees on a space-available basis; they are not a substitute for Medicare.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31, and California’s Medigap birthday rule gives you 60 days from your birthday each year to switch supplements without health questions.

Questions from NAS Lemoore & Edwards Air Force Base families

Frequently asked questions

I retired with TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit; we check whether one sold in your county is worth it.

Does Medicare pay at the Lemoore or Edwards clinic?

No. The base clinics see retirees on a space-available basis under TRICARE rules. Most retirees here pair TFL with civilian care at Adventist Health, Antelope Valley or Ridgecrest Regional.

I use the Fresno VA and do not have TRICARE. Do I need Part B?

In most cases, yes. VA care is not creditable for Part B; delaying it triggers a permanent penalty and leaves you uncovered at a non-VA hospital.

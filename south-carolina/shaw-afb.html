Medicare near Shaw Air Force Base (Sumter)

Why the Medicare choice is different for a Shaw family

Sumter County’s Medicare population includes thousands of Air Force retirees and spouses, and most of them reach 65 with TRICARE For Life waiting. TFL requires Medicare Parts A and B and then pays secondary to Medicare, so a Medigap policy is usually unnecessary; its pharmacy is creditable, so a Part D plan usually is too. What is left to decide is whether an MA-only Advantage plan’s dental, vision and hearing extras are worth having, and whether its network reaches Prisma Health Tuomey and the Columbia specialists you may need.

Veterans without TRICARE use the Columbia VA Health Care System, and for them the Part B timing rule is the one that matters: VA care is not creditable for Part B. The 20th Medical Group clinic on base continues to see retirees on a space-available basis; it is not a substitute for Medicare.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to South Carolina, losing a plan, or a FEMA hurricane declaration each open a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Shaw Air Force Base (Sumter) families

Frequently asked questions

I retired from Shaw with TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit; we check whether one is worth it for you.

I use the Columbia VA and do not have TRICARE. Do I need Part B?

In most cases, yes. VA care is not creditable for Part B; delaying it triggers a permanent penalty and leaves you uncovered at a non-VA hospital.

Can I keep using the Shaw clinic after 65?

Retirees can be seen at the 20th Medical Group on a space-available basis under TRICARE rules; Medicare does not pay there. Most Shaw retirees pair TFL with civilian care at Prisma Health Tuomey or in Columbia.

Medicare in Spartanburg

What to know before you compare plans in Spartanburg County

Spartanburg County offers a broad Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina and the full Part D lineup. Spartanburg Regional Healthcare System — Spartanburg Medical Center and its campuses — anchors the county and contracts with some plans and not others; Prisma and Bon Secours St. Francis in Greenville are close enough that many residents use them, and a plan built around Spartanburg Regional may not include them.

Spartanburg is also peach country and mill-village country, with a lot of retirees who worked for the manufacturers along the interstate and reach 65 with employer retiree coverage to coordinate. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code with your doctors and your other coverage in hand.

Care close to home

Major care in the area includes:

  • Spartanburg Medical Center (Spartanburg Regional)
  • Spartanburg Regional’s Mary Black campus
  • Pelham Medical Center (Greer)
  • Prisma Health and Bon Secours St. Francis (Greenville)
  • Columbia VA Health Care System (Spartanburg clinic)

Plan networks differ — we check that your doctors and hospital are covered before you enroll.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to South Carolina, losing a plan, or a FEMA hurricane declaration each open a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Spartanburg

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Spartanburg?

Spartanburg County typically offers a broad Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is Spartanburg Regional in my plan’s network?

Spartanburg Regional contracts with some Advantage plans and not others, changing year to year. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

Medicare in Hilton Head Island

What to know before you compare plans in Beaufort County

Beaufort County offers a broad Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina and the full Part D lineup. The island’s hospital, Hilton Head Hospital, became part of Novant Health in 2024; Beaufort Memorial is up the road in Beaufort, and many islanders see specialists in Savannah, which a South Carolina Advantage plan’s network may or may not include.

Almost everyone on Medicare here came from somewhere else. A Medigap policy from Connecticut or Ohio keeps working; an Advantage plan from there ended when you moved, which opens a Special Enrollment Period and a guaranteed-issue Medigap window that does not come back in a state with no birthday rule. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code with your doctors on both sides of the river.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to South Carolina, losing a plan, or a FEMA hurricane declaration each open a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Hilton Head Island

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available on Hilton Head Island?

Beaufort County typically offers a broad Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Can I see my Savannah doctors with a South Carolina plan?

With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy, yes. With a Medicare Advantage plan sold in Beaufort County, only if the Georgia provider is in that plan’s network, which we confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

Medicare in Beaufort

What to know before you compare plans in Beaufort County

Beaufort County offers a broad Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina and the full Part D lineup. Beaufort Memorial Hospital anchors the northern half of the county and contracts with some plans and not others; Novant’s Hilton Head hospital and MUSC in Charleston are the next stops for specialty care, and whether a plan’s network reaches them is the first thing we check.

A large share of Beaufort’s Medicare population is Marine Corps retirees from Parris Island and MCAS Beaufort with TRICARE For Life, for whom a Medigap policy and a Part D plan are usually redundant, plus veterans who use the Ralph H. Johnson VA’s Beaufort clinic. Naval Hospital Beaufort sees retirees on a space-available basis; Medicare does not pay there.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to South Carolina, losing a plan, or a FEMA hurricane declaration each open a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Beaufort

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Beaufort?

Beaufort County typically offers a broad Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in South Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

I retired from the Marine Corps with TRICARE For Life. Do I need anything besides Medicare A and B?

Usually not. TFL fills Medicare’s gaps and its pharmacy is creditable. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision; we check whether one is worth it and whether its network reaches your doctors.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

Medicare in Dayton, Nevada

On the Carson River between Carson City and Silver Springs, with care mostly a short drive west.

Dayton is one of Nevada's oldest settlements and one of Lyon County's fastest-growing communities, with a substantial retiree population. There is no hospital in town; most residents use Carson Tahoe Regional Medical Center in Carson City, about 12 miles west.

Lyon County · ZIP codes: 89403

A short drive, but a different county

You live in Lyon County and most of your care happens in Carson City. Plan availability follows Lyon County; usability follows the Carson City network. Both need checking.

The good news is that this is one of the easier versions of the problem — 12 miles is not 90, and most plans serving Lyon County contract reasonably in Carson City. It is still worth confirming rather than assuming.

Mound House

The Mound House area between Dayton and Carson City receives mail through Carson City's 89706 ZIP while much of it sits in Lyon County. If that is your address, confirm your county before enrolling.

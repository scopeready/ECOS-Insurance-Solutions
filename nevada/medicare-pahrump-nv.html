Medicare in Pahrump, Nevada

An hour from Las Vegas, and one of Nevada's fastest-growing retirement destinations.

Pahrump has drawn a steady stream of retirees out of southern California and the Las Vegas valley, and its Medicare-age population has grown accordingly. Desert View Hospital, a critical access hospital serving the community since 2006, provides emergency, surgical, imaging and rehabilitation care locally.

Nye County · ZIP codes: 89041, 89048, 89060, 89061

The Las Vegas question

Pahrump sits in Nye County, roughly 60 miles from Las Vegas over the Spring Mountains. Desert View handles a great deal, but specialty care and complex procedures generally mean a trip into the valley.

That makes one question decisive here: does the plan cover the Las Vegas specialists you would be referred to? You live in Nye County, which sets your plan menu — but a plan with a thin Clark County network is a poor fit no matter how good it looks locally.

Transportation

Given the drive, a plan's routine transportation benefit is worth more here than it would be in the valley. Ask how many trips a year are covered and whether a 60-mile each-way journey qualifies.

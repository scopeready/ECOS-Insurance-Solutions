Medicare Advantage (Part C)

Nevada has over 105 Medicare Advantage plans — including $0 premium options in nearly every county. Clark County alone offers 50+ plans. Many include extras well beyond what Original Medicare provides.

  • Many $0 premium plans across Nevada
  • Prescription drug coverage included
  • Dental, vision, and hearing
  • SilverSneakers, gym memberships
  • OTC allowances and transportation
  • Annual out-of-pocket maximum

Nevada carriers: Aetna Medicare, Alignment Health Plan, Anthem BCBS, Champion Health Plan, HealthSpring, Humana, SCAN Health Plan, Select Health, UnitedHealthcare, Wellcare

Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

Nevada offers 12 standardized Medigap plans. Unlike Advantage plans, Medigap lets you see any doctor who accepts Medicare — nationwide. Ideal for snowbirds who split time between Nevada and other states.

  • Any Medicare-accepting doctor, nationwide
  • No referrals for specialists
  • Covers copays, coinsurance, deductibles
  • Standardized plans (A through N)
  • Plan G and Plan N most popular in NV
  • Guaranteed renewable

Note: Requires separate Part D plan.

Medicare in Carson City, Nevada

The state capital, with the best provider access of anywhere in rural Nevada.

Carson City is a consolidated municipality — it functions as its own county for Medicare purposes. At roughly 144 square miles it is the smallest jurisdiction in Nevada by area, but it carries a deeper bench of doctors than anywhere else outside Las Vegas and Reno, anchored by Carson Tahoe Regional Medical Center.

Carson City (an independent city) · ZIP codes: 89701, 89703, 89706 (PO Box and government ZIPs: 89702, 89711–89714, 89721)

Watch your ZIP code

Mail addressed “Carson City 89705” — Jacks Valley and Indian Hills — is physically in Douglas County. The 89704 Washoe Valley area is in Washoe County. Plan eligibility follows your county, not the city on your envelope, so this is worth confirming before you enrol.

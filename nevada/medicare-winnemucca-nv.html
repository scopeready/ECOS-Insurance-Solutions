Medicare in Winnemucca, Nevada

North-central Nevada's hub on I-80, with a hospital that has served the area for over a century.

Winnemucca is the seat of Humboldt County and the main service centre for north-central Nevada. Humboldt General Hospital has served the area for more than a hundred years and provides emergency, inpatient and clinic care locally.

Humboldt County · ZIP codes: 89445, 89446

Like Elko, the local economy leans on mining, so a good number of residents are still working at 65 — which changes whether you should take Part B right away. More on that →

Reno for what stays out of reach

Humboldt General handles a lot, but specialty care generally means Reno, about 165 miles southwest on I-80. Check both the local network and the Washoe County network, and look closely at what a plan pays toward getting you there.

Not in the D-SNP service area

Humboldt County sits outside the 11-county Dual Special Needs Plan service area described elsewhere on this site. If you have both Medicare and Medicaid here, the options differ — worth a direct conversation. Nevada Medicaid and the Medicare Savings Programs →

Medicare in Fort Wayne

What to know before you compare plans in Allen County

Allen County offers a wide Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Indiana and the full Part D lineup. Parkview Health, anchored by Parkview Regional Medical Center on the north side, and Lutheran Health Network, anchored by Lutheran Hospital of Indiana on the southwest side with Dupont Hospital in the north, each contract with different carriers. Because patients drive in from Auburn, Columbia City, Huntington and Decatur, a plan’s network has to reach the whole northeast.

Fort Wayne also has one of the state’s larger veteran communities, served by the VA Northern Indiana Health Care System’s Fort Wayne campus; the Part B timing question for VA users is on our Veterans page. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code, confirm your doctors and pharmacy, and set Advantage beside a Medigap policy.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Already have a Medigap policy? Indiana’s birthday rule opens a switching window around your birthday each year.

Your options

Which kind of plan fits you in Fort Wayne?

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one Part C plans, often $0 premium, that use a network — so whether your doctors are IU Health, Ascension St. Vincent, Community, Franciscan, Parkview or Deaconess matters.

Medicare Supplement

Medigap pairs with Original Medicare and lets you see any provider nationwide that accepts Medicare — in Indianapolis, in Louisville or Chicago, or in Florida for the winter. Indiana now has a birthday rule for switching.

Part D drug plans

Standalone drug coverage chosen around your medications and pharmacy. 2026 out-of-pocket cap: $2,100. HoosierRx can help with the premium.

Questions from Fort Wayne

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Fort Wayne?

Allen County typically offers a wide Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in Indiana, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is Parkview or Lutheran in my plan’s network?

Each contracts with some Advantage plans and not others, changing year to year, and a plan built around one may leave the other out. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

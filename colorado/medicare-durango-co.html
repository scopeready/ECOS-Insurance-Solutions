Medicare in Durango, Colorado

Local Medicare guidance for Durango — plan availability, local care access, and what to check before you enroll.

Durango is gateway to Mesa Verde National Park and the San Juan Mountains, in southwest Colorado. Medicare plan availability here is set by La Plata County, and it changes every plan year.

Plan availability in La Plata County

Southwest Colorado's Medicare Advantage market is smaller than the Front Range, which puts more weight on checking network fit before you enroll.

Local care access

Mercy Regional Medical Center is Durango's hospital and the main referral center for a wide, sparsely populated region of southwest Colorado.

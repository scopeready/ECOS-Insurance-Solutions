Medicare Advantage vs. Supplement — Which Is Right for You?

Once you have Original Medicare (Parts A & B), you choose your path. We compare every option from 10 carriers — free.

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

All-in-one alternative to Original Medicare. Bundles hospital, medical, and usually drug coverage with extra benefits.

  • Many $0 premium plans available
  • Prescription drug coverage included
  • Dental, vision, and hearing
  • SilverSneakers, gym memberships
  • OTC allowances and transportation
  • Annual out-of-pocket maximum

Colorado carriers: Humana, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, Devoted Health, Kaiser Permanente, Select Health, Clear Spring Health, Wellcare, HealthSpring

Compare Advantage Plans →

Medicare Supplement (Medigap)

Works alongside Original Medicare. See any doctor who accepts Medicare — nationwide — no referrals needed.

  • Any Medicare-accepting doctor, nationwide
  • No referrals for specialists
  • Covers copays, coinsurance, deductibles
  • Standardized plans (A through N)
  • Plan G and Plan N most popular in CO
  • Guaranteed renewable

Note: Requires separate Part D plan.

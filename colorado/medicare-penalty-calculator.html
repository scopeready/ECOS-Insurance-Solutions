Medicare Late Enrollment Penalty Calculators

Delaying enrollment without qualifying coverage can mean permanent penalties on your monthly premiums — for life. Use these calculators to see the impact.

Part B Late Enrollment Penalty

10% surcharge for every full 12-month period you delayed Part B without qualifying employer coverage. Permanent.

Total months without Part B after your IEP, excluding qualifying employer group coverage.

Part D Late Enrollment Penalty

1% of the national base premium per uncovered month if you go 63+ days without creditable drug coverage. Permanent.

Months without Part D or creditable drug coverage after your initial period.

How to Avoid Medicare Penalties

Part B: Enroll during your IEP around age 65, or have qualifying employer group coverage (not COBRA, not Marketplace).

Part D: Enroll when first eligible or maintain creditable drug coverage (employer, VA, TRICARE). Get a creditable coverage letter.

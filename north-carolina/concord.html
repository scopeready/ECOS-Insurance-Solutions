Medicare in Concord

What to know before you compare plans in Cabarrus County

Cabarrus County offers a wide Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina and the full Part D lineup. Atrium Health Cabarrus, a 457-bed regional hospital in Concord, anchors local care and sits inside the Atrium network; Novant Health’s Charlotte hospitals and Levine Cancer Institute are a short drive down I-85.

Because Cabarrus care is mostly Atrium, an Advantage plan that includes Atrium usually works well here; one that does not can mean driving past your own hospital. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code and confirm before you enroll.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving into North Carolina, or a plan leaving your county, opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Concord?

Cabarrus County typically offers a wide Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is Atrium Health Cabarrus in my plan’s network?

It accepts Original Medicare and therefore any Medigap policy. It contracts with some Medicare Advantage plans and not others, and the list changes yearly. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

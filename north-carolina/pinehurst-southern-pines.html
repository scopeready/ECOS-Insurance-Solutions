Medicare in Pinehurst & Southern Pines

What to know before you compare plans in Moore County

Moore County offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina and the full Part D lineup. FirstHealth Moore Regional Hospital in Pinehurst, a 402-bed not-for-profit hospital, is the referral center for a 15-county region, and whether it is in a plan’s network decides most Sandhills comparisons; Duke, UNC and Cape Fear Valley take the tertiary referrals.

Because the Sandhills draw retirees from the Northeast, the Midwest and Fort Bragg alike, the first question we ask is what you are bringing with you: an Advantage plan from Ohio that will not work here, a Medigap policy that will, or TRICARE For Life. We compare what is sold at your new ZIP code and confirm FirstHealth before you enroll.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving into North Carolina, or a plan leaving your county, opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Pinehurst and Southern Pines?

Moore County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

I am moving to Pinehurst from another state. What happens to my Medicare plan?

Moving outside your Advantage or Part D plan’s service area opens a Special Enrollment Period to pick a plan sold in Moore County. A Medigap policy stays in force wherever you live, though the premium may be re-rated to North Carolina. See our Moving to North Carolina guide.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

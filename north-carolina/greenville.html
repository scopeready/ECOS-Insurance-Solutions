Medicare in Greenville

What to know before you compare plans in Pitt County

Pitt County offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina and the full Part D lineup. ECU Health Medical Center, formerly Vidant, is the largest employer in Eastern North Carolina and the referral center for its regional hospitals in Edenton, Ahoskie, Kenansville, Windsor, Washington and Tarboro; a plan that includes ECU Health works across the east, and one that does not can be a long drive to Raleigh.

Eastern North Carolina also has a high share of people with both Medicare and Medicaid, so Dual Special Needs Plans and the MQB programs are a bigger part of the menu here. We compare what is sold at your ZIP code and confirm your ECU Health doctors before you enroll.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving into North Carolina, or a plan leaving your county, opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Greenville?

Pitt County typically offers a moderate Medicare Advantage menu, every Medigap carrier in North Carolina, and the full Part D lineup, set by ZIP code and changing each plan year.

Is ECU Health in my plan’s network?

ECU Health accepts Original Medicare and therefore any Medigap policy. It contracts with some Medicare Advantage plans and not others, and the list changes yearly. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

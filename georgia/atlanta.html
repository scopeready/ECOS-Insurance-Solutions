Medicare in Atlanta

A lot of plans, one that's right for you

Metro Atlanta's density means an unusually large number of Medicare Advantage plans compete here, many at $0 premium — but they ride on different provider networks. With major systems like Emory, Piedmont, Northside, Grady, and Wellstar across the metro, the question that matters most is whether your doctors and hospital are in a given plan's network.

We help you cut through the noise: compare what's actually offered at your ZIP code, check your prescriptions, and weigh Advantage against a Medigap policy you can use with any Medicare provider nationwide.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31.

Questions from Atlanta

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Atlanta?

Availability changes by ZIP code, but most Atlanta residents can choose among several Medicare Advantage plans, a range of Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies, and standalone Part D drug plans. We compare what is offered at your address and what fits your doctors and medications.

Will my Emory or Piedmont doctors take a Medicare Advantage plan?

It depends on the specific plan's network. Before you enroll, we confirm whether your providers at Emory or Piedmont and your prescriptions are covered, so there are no surprises in January.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

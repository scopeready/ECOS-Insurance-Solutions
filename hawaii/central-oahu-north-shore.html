Medicare in the region

Central Oahu & the North Shore: what shapes the choice here

Central Oahu is plantation country turned suburb: Mililani’s planned neighborhoods, Wahiawa’s old town, and the North Shore’s small communities from Haleiwa to Kahuku. Residents have Honolulu County’s full Medicare menu, and most hospital care runs to Wahiawa General, Pali Momi in Aiea, or the big Honolulu hospitals down the H-2.

Schofield Barracks sits in the middle of it, and so do its retirees. For an Army family with TRICARE For Life, a Medigap policy and a Part D plan are usually redundant; for a veteran without TFL who uses the Matsunaga VA, the Part B timing rule is the one that matters. Our Schofield Barracks page walks through both.

Local plans to know: The menu is Honolulu County’s. What is different here is the military share: Schofield Barracks and Wheeler Army Airfield have filled Wahiawa and Mililani with Army retirees for generations, and TRICARE For Life changes what “best plan” means for them. Wahiawa General is the community hospital; Kahuku Medical Center is the North Shore’s critical-access hospital.

Counties: Honolulu (Central Oahu and the North Shore: Mililani, Mililani Mauka, Wahiawa, Whitmore Village, Waipio, Haleiwa, Waialua, Sunset Beach, Kahuku, Laie).

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to another island or losing a plan opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Which kind of plan fits you?

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one Part C plans, often $0 premium, that use a local network — in Hawaii that usually means choosing between Kaiser, HMSA and a few national carriers, island by island.

Medicare Supplement

Medigap pairs with Original Medicare and lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare — Queen’s, Straub, Hilo Benioff, or a specialist on the mainland.

Questions from Central Oahu

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Mililani and Wahiawa?

Honolulu County’s full menu: Kaiser Permanente, HMSA and national-carrier Advantage plans, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii, and all Part D plans. We confirm Wahiawa General, Pali Momi or your Kaiser clinic is in the network before you enroll.

I retired from the Army with TRICARE For Life. Do I need a Medigap policy or Part D?

Usually neither. TFL pays secondary to Medicare and its pharmacy is creditable drug coverage. An MA-only Advantage plan can add dental or vision without duplicating your drug benefit. Our Schofield Barracks page walks through it.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

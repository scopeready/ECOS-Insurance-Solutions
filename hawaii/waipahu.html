Medicare in Waipahu

What to know before you compare plans in Honolulu County

Waipahu’s Medicare menu is Honolulu County’s: Kaiser Permanente, HMSA and national-carrier Advantage plans, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii and the full Part D lineup. Hospital care runs to Pali Momi in Aiea or Queen’s West Oahu in Ewa Beach through HMSA and the national plans, or to Moanalua for Kaiser members. Many households here have three generations under one roof, and a lot of the questions we get come from adult children helping a parent.

We compare what is sold at your ZIP code, confirm your doctors and pharmacy, and check whether a Medicare Savings Program through Med-QUEST could pay the Part B premium, which is a real possibility for many Waipahu kupuna.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to another island or losing a plan opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Waipahu

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Waipahu?

Honolulu County typically offers Medicare Advantage plans from Kaiser Permanente, HMSA and national carriers, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii, and the full Part D lineup, set by county and changing each plan year.

Can Med-QUEST help pay my parent’s Part B premium?

Possibly. Hawaii’s Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, QI), run by Med-QUEST, pay the Part B premium for people with limited income and resources, and QMB also covers Medicare’s deductibles and copays. Apply at mybenefits.hawaii.gov or 1-800-316-8005; see our Med-QUEST page.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

Medicare in Kapaa

What to know before you compare plans in Kauai County

Kapaa and the North Shore have Kauai County’s Medicare menu: HMSA’s neighbor island Akamai Advantage plans and a few national carriers, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii and the full Part D lineup, with no Kaiser Senior Advantage on the island. Samuel Mahelona Memorial Hospital, a public critical-access hospital with a 24-hour emergency department and most of its beds in long-term care, is the East Side’s hospital; Wilcox Medical Center in Lihue is the island’s main hospital.

Princeville and Hanalei retirees are often mainland transplants, and the same rule applies as everywhere else in Hawaii: a Medigap policy from home keeps working, an Advantage HMO from home generally does not, and changing your legal residence opens a Special Enrollment Period. We help make the move cleanly.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to another island or losing a plan opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one Part C plans, often $0 premium, that use a local network — in Hawaii that usually means choosing between Kaiser, HMSA and a few national carriers, island by island.

Questions from Kapaa

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Kapaa?

Kauai County typically offers HMSA’s neighbor island Akamai Advantage plans and a small number of national-carrier Advantage plans, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii, and the full Part D lineup. Kaiser Senior Advantage is not sold on Kauai.

I just moved to Princeville from the mainland. What do I do about my plan?

Changing your legal residence to Kauai County opens a Special Enrollment Period to choose from the plans sold here. A Medigap policy from home usually keeps working, though the premium may be re-rated; an Advantage plan from home ends when you move. We handle both directions.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

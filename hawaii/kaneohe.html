Medicare in Kaneohe

What to know before you compare plans in Honolulu County

Kaneohe has Honolulu County’s full Medicare menu: Kaiser Permanente, HMSA and national-carrier Advantage plans, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii and the full Part D lineup. Adventist Health Castle in Kailua is the Windward side’s hospital and contracts through HMSA and the national plans; Kaiser members cross to Moanalua. Whether a plan includes Castle and the Honolulu specialists you would be referred to is the first thing we check.

Marine Corps Base Hawaii shapes the rest of the town. Retired Marines and sailors with TRICARE For Life usually need neither a Medigap policy nor a Part D plan; the question is whether an MA-only Advantage plan’s extras are worth it. See our Marine Corps Base Hawaii page.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to another island or losing a plan opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Your options

Which kind of plan fits you in Kaneohe?

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one Part C plans, often $0 premium, that use a local network — in Hawaii that usually means choosing between Kaiser, HMSA and a few national carriers, island by island.

Medicare Supplement

Medigap pairs with Original Medicare and lets you see any provider that accepts Medicare — Queen’s, Straub, Hilo Benioff, or a specialist on the mainland.

Questions from Kaneohe

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available in Kaneohe?

Honolulu County typically offers Medicare Advantage plans from Kaiser Permanente, HMSA and national carriers, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii, and the full Part D lineup, set by county and changing each plan year.

Is Adventist Health Castle in my plan’s network?

Castle contracts with some Advantage plans and not others, changing year to year. With Original Medicare and a Medigap policy there is no network. We confirm before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.

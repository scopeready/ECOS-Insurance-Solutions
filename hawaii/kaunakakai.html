Medicare in Kaunakakai (Molokai)

What to know before you compare plans in Maui County

Molokai’s Medicare menu is Maui County’s minus Kaiser: Kaiser Permanente Senior Advantage’s service area covers the island of Maui but not Molokai, so the Advantage choice here is HMSA’s neighbor island Akamai Advantage plans and whichever national carriers sell in the county that year, alongside every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii and the full Part D lineup. Molokai General Hospital in Kaunakakai, part of the Queen’s system, is the island’s only hospital; Molokai Community Health Center is its federally qualified health center; and the VA provides a physician at the Molokai Rural Health Center three days a week.

Complex cases leave the island by air. A Medigap policy with Original Medicare covers Queen’s Punchbowl or Maui Memorial without a referral or travel rule; an HMSA neighbor island plan includes interisland travel help when HMSA refers you off-island. We read exactly what that covers before you enroll.

When you can enroll

Turning 65? Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7 months around your birthday. Already on Medicare? The Annual Election Period is Oct 15–Dec 7, and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs Jan 1–Mar 31. Moving to another island or losing a plan opens a Special Enrollment Period.

Questions from Kaunakakai (Molokai)

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare plans are available on Molokai?

Maui County’s menu without Kaiser: HMSA’s neighbor island Akamai Advantage plans and any national carriers selling in the county that year, every Medigap company licensed in Hawaii, and the full Part D lineup. Kaiser Senior Advantage’s service area does not include Molokai.

Almost all of my specialty care is on Oahu. Which plan covers that?

Original Medicare with a Medigap policy covers any provider that accepts Medicare on any island or the mainland, with no referral and no travel rule. HMSA’s neighbor island Akamai Advantage plans include help with interisland travel when HMSA refers you to a participating specialist on another island; Kaiser refers within its own system. We read the plan’s travel rules with you before you enroll.

How much does your help cost?

Nothing. Independent agents are paid by the carriers when you enroll, so comparing plans and reviewing your coverage each year is free to you.
